Introduction to All That Matters

Literally everything in nutrition science can be explained by simple physics: your stomach can only hold so much at a time.

Focusing on individual foods, magical properties of the foods (such as macronutrients, micronutrients, etc.) is a complete waste of time.

Very nearly, all of America's health problems are caused by excess calorie intake.

If you want to be healthy, you have to limit your calorie intake.

If you want to limit your calorie intake without going insane, you have to focus on the physical properties of the food you are eating.

The most important things for eating foods that limit consumption are:

  1. Calorie density: How many calories per gram of food? Since your stomach can only hold so many grams of food, the lower the number of calories per gram, the lower the calorie intake.
  2. Grams eaten per minute: If you sat there and ate the food with a spoon (or however you eat it), how fast would you eat it? The slower, the better.
